Mech Destiny

Privacy policy

1. Who we are

Mech Destiny is a merge-parts battle game built for the Pi Next Hackathon 2026 by team velt. Contact: aditzgraha@gmail.com. App URL: https://mech-destiny-prod.vercel.app/.

2. What data we collect — and why

  • Pi account identity: when you sign in with Pi, we receive your Pi user id (uid) and username from Pi Authentication. We use the uid as your account key (machine, parts, streaks, battles, leaderboard) and the username only to display your leaderboard entry. We do not collect your email, password, or any other login credential — Pi Authentication is the only sign-in method.
  • Game activity: daily check-ins, parts collected/merged/battled/traded, Blueprint Fragments, draws, shop listings and trade offers, and Pi payments you make inside the app (payment id, amount in Pi, completion status). This is the app itself: without it there is no machine, no battle, and no shop.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (request time, app version) for debugging and abuse prevention. We do not run third-party trackers or advertising SDKs in this version.

3. What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your Pi wallet passphrase, private key, or Server API Key (that key lives only on our server). We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. Pi payments are processed by Pi Network; we receive only the payment id, amount, and completion status needed to grant your draws and shop purchases.

4. How data is stored and how long we keep it

Game data is stored in the app's database hosted on Vercel's infrastructure (Vercel KV / Upstash Redis). We keep your machine and leaderboard data while your account is active and for 12 months after your last visit, then delete it. Server logs are kept for 30 days.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the data we hold about your Pi uid by contacting aditzgraha@gmail.com. Deleting your data removes your machine, parts, and leaderboard entries — they cannot be restored. You can also stop using the app at any time; Pi sign-in can be revoked from your Pi account settings.

6. Children

Mech Destiny is intended for users aged 18 and over, in line with the Pi Next Hackathon eligibility rules.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and note the new "last updated" date below. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
