Daily check-in

One check-in per UTC day: +50 points, plus +10 per streak day (cap +70), and +1 random part. Streak milestones: day 3 → Rare, day 7 → Epic, day 14 → Legendary. Miss a day and your streak resets to day 1. Your first check-in grants your Base Machine.

Your machine & parts

Every part has randomly rolled Power / Speed / Armor. Merge 2 identical parts (same type and rarity) into 1 part, one rarity up. Tick two identical parts, then merge. Inventory cap: 50 parts.

Sign in or try as guest to see your parts.

Battle — absolute stakes

One free battle a day. First you see the enemy — their power and stats — then you choose: fight, reroll the opponent once, or (after the reroll) surrender for −25 points and keep your parts. Lose and you lose a part. Win and you take one — plus a Blueprint Fragment (2 on a 3-win streak). Ethereal never drops from battle.

The Forge — craft an Ethereal

5 Blueprint Fragments + 1 Legendary part → 1 Ethereal of that type, with a rolled ability (Overdrive, Shield, or Magnet). Ethereal is never a free or battle drop. Max 3 Ethereals owned, max 3 equipped.

Pay draws — Ethereal Fate

Paid draws never drop Commons — minimum is Rare. Standard Draw 0.5 π (Rare 70 / Epic 22 / Legendary 7 / Ethereal 1%), Ethereal Forge 1 π (Rare 55 / Epic 30 / Legendary 12 / Ethereal 3%), 10-pack 9 π (Forge rates, guaranteed at least 1 Legendary). 10% of every draw goes to the weekly reward pool.

How it works

  1. Open this app in Pi Browser and sign in with Pi.
  2. Check in once a day — day 1 gives you a Base Machine and a part; day 3, 7 and 14 give guaranteed Rare, Epic and Legendary parts.
  3. Merge identical parts to climb rarities. Battle once a day — first you see the enemy's power, then you fight, reroll once, or surrender (−25 pts, keep your parts) after the reroll. The loser loses a part.
  4. Forge Ethereals from 5 Blueprint Fragments + a Legendary, or try a pay draw (minimum Rare, Ethereal Fate pity applies).
  5. Sell duplicates and trade in the shop. Climb the weekly match ranking for a share of the reward pool.
