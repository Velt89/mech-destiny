Mech Destiny

Terms of Service

Mech Destiny is a merge-parts battle game built for the Pi Next Hackathon 2026 by team velt. Contact: aditzgraha@gmail.com. App URL: https://mech-destiny-prod.vercel.app/. By using the app you accept these terms.

1. The game

You sign in with Pi Authentication and build a machine from parts (Gear, Circuit, Battery, Magnet, Core) in five rarities: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Ethereal. Parts come from daily check-ins, merges, battles, and paid draws. Battles are against bot rivals in this version. Your machine, parts, streaks, and leaderboard entries are stored on our server and keyed to your Pi user id.

2. Pi payments

Paid draws and shop purchases are paid in Pi through Pi Network's payment flow. All payments inside the app are in Pi only. Draw odds are shown in the app before you pay: draws never drop below Rare, and Ethereal parts are rare by design. Paid draws are final — Pi spent on a draw cannot be refunded, because the drawn part is delivered immediately. Shop listings are player-to-player: the seller sets the price, and the buyer pays in Pi through the app.

3. Reward pool

10% of every paid draw goes into the weekly reward pool. Each week the top 3 players in the match ranking split 10% of the total pool (1st 5%, 2nd 3%, 3rd 2%). Pool payouts are sent in Pi to the winners' Pi wallets by the app. We aim to pay winners promptly each week; exact timing may vary while the game is in its hackathon version.

4. Fair play

One account per person. Do not use bots, scripts, multiple accounts, or any exploit to farm check-ins, draws, battles, or leaderboard positions. We may remove parts, points, or leaderboard entries — or suspend an account — if we find abuse. Losing a battle can cost you a part; that is part of the game, and lost parts are not restored.

5. Availability and changes

Mech Destiny is a hackathon project and may change: game balance, odds, caps, and features can be adjusted, and the service may be interrupted for maintenance. We will keep the app running as best we can, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted availability, and we are not liable for lost game progress or lost Pi caused by interruptions, bugs, or changes to the game.
